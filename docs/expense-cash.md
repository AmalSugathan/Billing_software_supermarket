# Expense and cash increment

Build paid expenses, store-scoped cash/bank/UPI/card accounts, immutable money movements, explicitly confirmed receipts/withdrawals and expense reversals. Account opening amounts are balance evidence, not revenue or current-period cash flow. Cashier sessions use a terminal-linked drawer account. Opening count must reconcile with its recorded balance. Closing snapshots expected/actual cash and requires a reason for variance; a separate variance movement reconciles the next opening without labelling a shortage as an expense or fraud. Sessions and closing records are append-only, and closed sessions never accept further movements. POS receipts/refunds will use this boundary in the next increment; no pretend sales are created now.

Schema: financial_account, cash_session, financial_movement, expense, expense_reversal and cash_closing with tenant/store/account/session foreign keys, forced RLS, immutable triggers and indexes. Movement inserts lock the account, reject insufficient funds/overflow and closed drawer sessions. Repeated commands use tenant/key/hash identity; all documents, movements and audit commit together. Cash opening/session closure have explicit human confirmation. Expense reversal and manual account funding/withdrawal require owner actions.approve; ordinary paid expense entry uses expenses.manage. A new cash.sessions capability is migrated into existing OWNER/STORE_MANAGER/CASHIER roles and used for new businesses.

Frontend: Expenses and Cash & bank modules, assigned-store selection, account creation, paid expense entry/reversal, manual money movements, cashier session opening/closing, and recorded account/expense/movement views. All money travels as decimal strings. Confirmation is cleared by edits; failed-response retries retain a key. Synthetic development records stay separate from real bills. No bank integration, supplier payment, GST credit claim, unpaid expense, double-entry financial statements, transfers, POS, complete profit or offline durability is claimed by this increment.

## Implemented operation
In Cash & bank, an owner adds accounts and selects an existing terminal for each cash drawer. Record verified/synthetic-demo opening funds explicitly; nothing is seeded automatically. A cashier counts and opens the drawer, with at most one open session per cashier/business and per account. If a pre-opening count differs, the owner uses Reconcile cash before opening to preserve the old balance and record an explained variance. Receipts/withdrawals require owner confirmation and are not substitutes for POS sales/refunds.

In Expenses, enter a unique store reference, source date, category, description, paid amount and payment account. Cash expenses require an open drawer session; bank/UPI/card expenses affect only their selected account. Reversing an expense requires actual returned funds or a confirmed correction basis; its original row stays visible and the linked compensating entry shows it as reversed. A cash reversal after a previous session has closed must use a new open session on that same account.

Close a cashier session after counting actual cash. A stale expected amount blocks closing until refresh/review. Explain every difference; the recorded variance movement adjusts the drawer to its actual count without describing the difference as an expense or revenue. Original opening/closing counts and audit remain unchanged. POST commands require decimal strings, explicit confirmation and retry UUIDs. UI keys are retained while mounted; this does not satisfy offline/crash durability.

Development tests use labelled synthetic funds/expenses. Original bills and the user's existing account/business are not used as test transactions. The existing demo daily scenario contains POS sale/refund inputs for later work; this increment does not pretend those sales/refunds have occurred. No supplier-payment allocation, bank reconciliation, unpaid expenses, GST credit eligibility, complete financial statements or live-store readiness is claimed.
